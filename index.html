Commissions

Commission information

Available Work

Character art, comic covers, promotional illustrations, giveaway art, and selected custom requests.

Typical Process

Inquiry, quote, sketch approval, final rendering, delivery, and optional public showcase.

Best For

Clients who can provide a short brief, references, intended usage, and a preferred deadline.

Turnaround

Smaller requests move faster. Multi-part illustration or comic work needs more time and scheduling.

Terms of Service

Basic commission terms

Payment

Projects begin after agreement on price, scope, and schedule.

Revisions

Small revisions are easiest during sketch review before the final rendering stage.

Usage

Commercial and personal usage rights should be clearly agreed before work begins.

Cancellations

If a project stops midway, completed work and time already spent still count toward the agreement.
